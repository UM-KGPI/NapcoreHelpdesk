#### NAPCORE at the Transport Research Arena 2026
NAPCORE actively participated in the Transport Research Arena (TRA) 2026 in Budapest.
The NAPCORE community hosted key discussions at the Federal Highway Research Institute (BASt) stand. The event served as a major hub for networking, insight-sharing, and exploring the future of National Access Points across Europe. Highlights from the session included a presentation on Alternative Fuels Data by András Selmeczy and Timo Hoffmann at the FEHRL Transport Research Village, alongside an interactive “Meet the Expert” session with Timo Hoffmann at the German pavilion.
Then, Stephen T’Siobbel highlighted the results of technical paper accepted: “Advancing Mobility Data Interoperability: TN-ITS’s Breakthroughs in Standards Alignment, Quality Frameworks, and Opportunities for AI-Driven Tools Development” during his intervention Advancing Mobility Data Interoperability: TN-ITS’s Breakthroughs.
He explored the synergy between TN-ITS and NAPCORE focusing on three major outcomes:
- Strategic alignment of TN-ITS with DATEX II
- Refinement of TN-ITS technical specifications
- Development of a holistic data quality framework
