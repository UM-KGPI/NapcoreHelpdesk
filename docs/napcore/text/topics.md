#### NAPCORE brings together not only Europe’s National Access Points (NAPs) but also mobility data stakeholders and domain experts to strengthen the harmonisation, accessibility, and usability of transport and mobility data across the continent.
#### The Topics section provides an overview of the thematic areas where NAPCORE is actively driving alignment, improving standards, and supporting the deployment of high‑quality mobility data services.
#### Quality
Ensuring data quality is one of NAPCORE’s fundamental missions. This section highlights our efforts to harmonise quality frameworks across Europe, define shared indicators, support NAP operators, and promote reliable, machine‑readable datasets that unlock user‑oriented mobility services.
→ Learn more about Quality
#### Cycling & Walking
Active mobility is a key pillar of sustainable transport. Here, we explore how better data on cycling and walking networks, services, and conditions can fuel safer, smarter, and more attractive active travel across Europe. This includes harmonising data models, supporting cross‑border interoperability, and fostering collaboration between authorities, operators, and innovators.
