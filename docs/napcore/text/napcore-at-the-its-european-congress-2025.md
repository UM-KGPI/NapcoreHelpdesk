#### Proceedings of the European ITS Congress in Seville
#### Seville, Spain May 19-21, 2025
NAPCORE participated in the ITS Congress with a fairly large and interactive booth.
It was an opportunity for an informal meeting and networking time to reconnect with our members.
During the Congress, it was possible to celebrate the achievements of NAPCORE and also to start talking about the new future activities of NAPCORE-X.
The sessions that NAPCORE held, which are the result of the work done, are listed below.
For each session, subdivided by days, there is a presentation that you can download.
