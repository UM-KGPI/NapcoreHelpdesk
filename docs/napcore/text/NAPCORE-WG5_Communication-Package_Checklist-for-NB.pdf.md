Checklist for National Bodies to efficiently support stakeholders affected by the
Delegated Regulations (EU)

This document provides a checklist for National Bodies/Competent Authorities to consult in order to
efficiently support stakeholders affected by the Delegated Regulations (EU) of the ITS Directive
2010/40/EU (namely Delegated Regulation (EU) No 885/2013, ( EU) No 886/2013, (EU) 2015/962 and
(EU) 2017/1926). The checklist contains items that should ideally be provided by each National Body.
☐ Contact address
✓ E-mail address stakeholders can reach out to if they have questions, require consultation or
additional information and to submit self -declarations. The mailbox should be regularly
checked by the National Body.
☐ A dedicated website for the National Body
✓ Each National Body is advised to have a website where information on the Delegated
Regulations (EU), the obligations for data and service providers, the NAPCORE
recommendations on Compliance Assessment as well as forms and templates for Compliance
Assessment (ideally, translated into the national language(s)) can be accessed. The website
should also refer to the aforementioned contact address and include links to the NAP or be
integrated into the NAP itself.
☐ Harmonised forms and templates for Compliance Assessment
✓ The harmonised forms and templates for Compliance Assessment developed within the
NAPCORE project (self -declaration forms, accompanying documents, Compliance
Assessment forms, Compliance Assessment result forms) could be provided via the website
of each National Body. Ideally, these documents will be translated to the national language(s)
of the respective country. Alternatively, links to the NAPCORE website can be provided.
☐ Guidelines for filling the forms and templates
✓ In order to facilitate the process of filling -in and submitting self -declarations and
accompanying documents, the National Bodies can provide guidelines for filling templates
and filled-in sample documents.
☐ Information on data formats
✓ It would be advisable for National Bodies to provide data and service providers with
information on which data categories mentioned in the Delegated Regulations should be
provided on the NAPs in which format. For this purpose, a table covering all the relevant data
categories and the corresponding formats could be created. It is moreover recommended to
provide links with additional information about data formats and converter tools.
☐ FAQs
✓ Answers to frequently asked questions from data and service providers could also be made
available on the website. The FAQs developed within the NAPCORE project can be used,
translated and adapted to national conditions.

☐ Social Media
✓ In addition to the website, the National Bodies can be active on social media (e.g. LinkedIn)
in order to increase their outreach to stakeholders and call attention to recent developments
or measures to increase stakeholder engagement.
✓ They could communicate about their activities, their involvement in international meetings,
the development of the NAP, etc.
☐ Stakeholder lists
✓ Per Delegated Regulation, the National Bodies are advised to keep a list with identified
stakeholders (and respective contact details) affected by obligations lied out therein. This list
should be regularly kept up to date.
☐ Info mailing
✓ NAPCORE recommends contacting s takeholders identified to be affected by the Delegated
Regulations by e-mail and informing them about the obligations and requirements lied out
in the Delegated Regulations. As part of this process, it is also possible to check whether the
respective stakeholders are affected by the Delegated Regulations at all.
☐ Organisation of stakeholder events
✓ It is recommended for National Bodies to organise one to two stakeholder events (virtually
or in-person) per year in order to inform stakeholders about the Delegated Regulations, the
requirements and obligations for sharing data and the Compliance Assessment process.
☐ Chat feature
✓ A chat feature (chat bot or live chat) can be established at the National Bodies’ websites to
clarify questions data or service providers might have in a low-threshold manner.
☐ One-on-one meetings
✓ If requested by the stakeholder, the National Bodies can carry out one-on-one meetings with
the stakeholder to consult them on their questions.
