#### An advising body for solicited and unsolicited advice
#### The Advisory Board is an advising body for solicited and unsolicited advice to the NAPCORE Steering Committee and NAPCORE activities regarding the project and its interactions in the European ITS ecosystem.
The main task of the Advisory Board is to give overall non-binding strategic advice to serve the common objectives and vision of NAPCORE. Members of the Advisory Board should:
- represent a multi-national stakeholder group with a European interest or
- be renowned experts having a known status in a relevant network,
- represent one or multiple key sectors related to the Delegated Regulations covered by NAPCORE,
- have the ability to communicate sectoral positions to NAPCORE and vice-versa,
- be available to participate in regular meetings and consultations.
