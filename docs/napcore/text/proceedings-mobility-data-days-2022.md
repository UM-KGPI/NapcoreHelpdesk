#### PROCEEDINGS
Mobility Data Days 2022
3-4 November
Paris La Défense
#### The first event of NAPCORE registered 200 delegates from 24 countries. The key elements that have led to the success of the event have been the sharing of the knowledge together with the willingness to put every expertise at disposal of colleagues.
All session organizers contributed to this page providing the presentations of their own sessions.
The goal of this section is:
– to collect all the material presented at the 17 sessions organized (this activity is still on-going) https://napcore.eu/documents/PROCEEDINGS MDD 2022.zip
– to share the videos registered during the Opening and the Closing sessions
