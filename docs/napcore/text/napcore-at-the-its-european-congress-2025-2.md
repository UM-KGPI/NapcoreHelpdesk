#### NAPCORE at the ITS European Congress 2025
#### Seville, Spain 19-21 May 2025
Booth D3
NAPCORE will participate at next ITS Congress with a fairly large and interactive booth.
The idea is to meet all the interested people and reconnect with our members. It will be a moment of informal get together and networking.
But the Congress will be also the place where celebrating NAPCORE achievements.
And there will the possibility to start talking and to discuss the topics, we will be working on
NAPCORE-X’s new activities.
Once there, visit us at booth D3 and join the sessions below to get an insight into what
NAPCORE has been up to (and will be working on in the future)!
Meet us in Seville!
