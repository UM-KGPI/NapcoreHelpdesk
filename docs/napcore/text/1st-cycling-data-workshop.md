#### 1st cycling data workshop
#### 13th September 9:30-12:00
#### You can register HERE
In May Taskforce cycling data of NAPCORE had a closed workshop on use cases for cycling.
In the first workshop the Taskforce wants to proceed on this and tell you about what they collected so far and further explore together what is missing and finding the corresponding data types necessary for these use cases.
Agenda
9:30-9:40 Welcome and introduction
9:40-10:00 Results of workshop on 22nd May
10:00-10:30 Complimentary use cases
10:30-10:40 short break
10:40-10:45 explanation of finding data types for the use cases
10:45-11:30 subgroups and data types
11:30-11:50 sharing data types for use cases
11:50-12:00 wrapping up and what’s next
After this workshop Taskforce cycling data of NAPCORE wants to talk further on certain data types to find the necessity of a standard and the functional requirements to make it usable for the different use cases they found together.
So, block in your agenda and sent this dates to people in your network!
7th October 15:00-17:00 “Workshop on Functional requirements on parking data for cycling”
16th October 10:00-12:00 “Workshop on Functional requirements on counting data for cycling”
17th October 10:00-12:00 “Workshop on Functional requirements on real time cycling data”
17th October 14:00-16:00 “Workshop on Functional requirements on infrastructure data for cycling” Thursday
Taskforce cycling data of NAPCORE hopes to see at (one of) these workshops!
