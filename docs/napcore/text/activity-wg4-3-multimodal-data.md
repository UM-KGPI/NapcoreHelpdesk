M4.3.5 Technical mapping of multimodal data standards and recommendations
This milestone focuses on the technical mapping of parking data standards. To achieve this, the existing NeTEx parking national profiles currently used in European countries (France and Italy) were analysed.
Additionally, the report provides a technical mapping of the elements from various standards used in the parking domain, specifically the NeTEx national parking profiles and APDS.
The document concludes with a series of recommendations and next steps to enrich the parking standards.
