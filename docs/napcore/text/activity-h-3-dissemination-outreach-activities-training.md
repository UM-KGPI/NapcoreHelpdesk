MH.3.3 Mobility Data Days 2022 – Paris – PROCEEDINGS
The first event of NAPCORE registered 200 delegates from 24 countries. The key elements that have led to the success of the event have been the sharing of the knowledge together with the willingness to put every expertise at disposal of colleagues.
The goal of this section is:
– to collect all the material presented at the 17 sessions organized
– to share the videos registered during the Opening and the Closing sessions
MH.3.4 Mobility Data Days 2023 – Budapest – PROCEEDINGS
The key numbers of the second event of NAPCORE:
– 219 registered delegates
– 27 countries represented
– 4 Strategic Sessions
– 21 technical sessions
Main positive aspects:
– the presence of several representatives for every Member State
– the attendance of many non NAPCORE members
– different possibilities of networking
– the professional organization and hospitality
– the location with conference rooms and night stay

MH.3.8 2023 Dissemination activity report
This document aims to collect all the contributions produced by H.3 team in cooperation with entire NAPCORE Groups and Sub-working groups regarding the Dissemination of results of the project.
The paper refers to the period from 1st January 2023 to 31st December 2023.
The content of the Dissemination Report – activities that provide publicity for NAPCORE – is created by all
members through their commitment in the activities, for example by technical papers concerning Milestones and Deliverables achieved, or by their promotion of these results during workshops, congresses, meetings.
Other important visibility of the project is achieved through articles in newspapers, posts on LinkedIn, highlights on the website and organization of webinars and trainings.
