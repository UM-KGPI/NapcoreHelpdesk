#### NAPCORE Alternative Fuels – 2nd Workshop
On the 25th of September the second NAPCORE Workshop on AFIR data will be held online. Organised in cooperation with the European Commission and with contributions from the EVRoaming Foundation, EAFO (FIER), POLIS and several OEM and end-user orientated operators.
During the workshop we aim to cover the latest updates in the development of the Common European Access Point with first glimps of how this will work in practise. In this session we will take a deeper look at hydrogen as alternative fuel and how this is to be adopted into a National Access Point.
In this 2nd workshop we will also have ample input from market players in the field of EV charging and end-user applications. Providing us with information on what AFIR means for their work and development and what is still challenging to understand or lack all together.
These workshops are aimed at understanding the regulations and their impacts better, discussing important points with the whole Alternative Fuels community, and enhancing the capabilities of all to drive towards sustainable mobility. The program for the workshop foresees also two discussing rounds enabling attendees to ask questions and raise points that need all of our attention.
We look forward to seeing you all in this workshop. If you haven’t yet received an invite, you can register for attendance here, and we will make sure that you are able to join this online session.
