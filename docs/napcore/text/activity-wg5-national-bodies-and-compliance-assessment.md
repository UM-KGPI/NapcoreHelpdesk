M5.4 Quality and evaluation criteria defined
This report’s primary objective is to establish unified quality and evaluation criteria for Compliance Assessment concerning traffic data. One key outcome of WG5’s work on this topic was the differentiation of quality criteria based on data type—dynamic and static—and event type—planned and unplanned events. WG5’s findings underscore the necessity of a collaborative approach to motivate stakeholders to provide data for NAPs, aiming to maximize synergies and minimize the risk of multiple contacts and potential conflicting messages.
M5.6 Strategies and actions to motivate organisations to provide data on the NAP
This report provides a detailed exploration of initial considerations regarding strategies and actions aimed at encouraging (private, international) organizations to contribute data to NAPs. The increasing number of stakeholders, coupled with the expansion of the relevant mobility network, poses a growing challenge in motivating data provision. To tackle this issue, a variety of measures, including organizational, communicational, and strategic initiatives, are essential. The results of WG5’s efforts on this matter emphasize the need for a unified approach in motivating stakeholders to contribute data to NAPs. This approach is crucial not only to maximize synergies but also to minimize the risk of multiple contacts and potential contradictory messages.
M5.9 Development of National Body Reference Architecture
This report conveys the analysis and development toward a reference architecture which brings all NAPCORE WG5 National Body harmonization activities together.
As work on harmonizing National Bodies is ongoing, this document presents the initial version of the architecture.
Updates to the National Body Reference Architecture in 2024 will incorporate new developments, including adjustments related to the new release of the ITS directive, based on inputs from WG5.
M5.10 European National Bodies cooperation – Prototypical process for multi-national self-declarations and compliance assessment
This document describes a support system (named ENBOSS – European National Body Support System) that allows for Service Providers and Data Providers to submit self-declarations in a central system in case their service or data is covering multiple countries. In addition, this system is designed to support the National Bodies in coordinating a joint compliance assessment of randomly selected self-declarations. This approach is meant to decrease the effort needed on both sides – the providers and the National Bodies.
The updates and releases can be found at: https://github.com/NAPCORE/NB-Reference-Architecture
