## DATEX II packages addressed by the RRP The types of data on infrastructure
### Location of service areas and rest areas
The reference profile also includes the minimum information needed to identify the location of service and rest areas, typically situated along motorways. These are designated places where drivers can stop to rest and access available services and amenities.
Note: Safe and Secure Truck Parking Areas (SSTPAs) are not explicitly included in this definition, as they fall under the scope of Delegated Regulation (EU) 885/2015. However, they may be addressed either as a specific subset of rest areas or separately, depending on their operational context.
There is no reference profile in DATEX II for locations of service areas and rest areas.
