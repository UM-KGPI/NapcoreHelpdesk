## EXPERT
When you are an expert in DATEX II you are able to create your own publications and extensions. This level is aimed at experts on (functional) content modelling.
While the previous level focused on smaller extensions, this level addresses the more complex extensions and new publications in the UML model. This includes the methodology on making extensions, choosing the right approach in making more complex extensions, re-using existing parts of the model, and sharing extensions. This level also dives deeper into packaging, explaining how you can assemble packages to a deployable set of schema files. Clear examples are clarifying this process step by step. Required skills are advanced knowledge of information modelling and UML and enterprise architect.
With knowledge of this level at your disposal you are able to create a DATEX II information model autonomously fully tailored to your demand.
