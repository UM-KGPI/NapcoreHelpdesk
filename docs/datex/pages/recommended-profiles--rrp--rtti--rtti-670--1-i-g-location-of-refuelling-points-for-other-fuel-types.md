## DATEX II packages addressed by the RRP The types of data on infrastructure
### Location of refuelling points and stations for all other fuel types
The reference profile defines the minimum required information to disseminate the geographic location of refuelling stations and refuelling points that provide fuel types other than those already addressed (e.g., gasoline, diesel, biodiesel).
Each station must include at least one refuelling point, capable of refuelling one vehicle at a time. The minimum dataset must include:
The precise location of the infrastructure (including individual refuelling points)
- Contact details
- Opening hours
- Accepted payment methods
- Permitted vehicle characteristics (e.g. size, weight)
- The specific type(s) of fuel provided
For larger facilities or those with specific access constraints—such as stations along motorways—entry and exit points should be specified separately to support accurate navigation and accessibility.
